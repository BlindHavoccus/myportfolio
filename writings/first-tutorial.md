# My first tutorial: working with light

*This is a placeholder article. Replace it with your own text, or publish a PDF instead.*

Light decides most of a drawing before the first line is made. In this tutorial I walk through how I plan where the light comes from, and how I mark the shadows before I commit to any detail.

## Step 1: Choose the light source

Decide where the light is coming from, and stick to it. Changing the light halfway through is the most common reason a drawing looks confused.

- Pick one light direction.
- Mark it with a small arrow in the corner of the page.
- Keep that arrow visible until the drawing is finished.

## Step 2: Block in the shadows

Use a soft pencil for the big shapes. Work from the darkest shadow outward, so the highlights are left for last.

1. Lightly sketch the main shapes.
2. Fill the shadow side with a single even tone.
3. Lift the highlights with an eraser.

Pencil grades matter here. A **6B** gives deep darks, and an *HB* keeps the edges clean.

## Step 3: Check the result

Look at the drawing in a mirror, or photograph it in black and white. Problems that hide in colour show up immediately in greyscale.
